The game's 3D models, named by what they are. Meshes generated from a concept image with
TRELLIS.2 (Microsoft, MIT License), using the DINOv3 image encoder (Meta, DINOv3 License) and
BiRefNet background removal (ZhengPeng7, MIT License), then cleaned up, painted and rigged in Blender.
Each .blend records this as the scene custom property `source`.

Concept images come from Qwen-Image-2512 (Apache-2.0), never Qwen-Image-2.1 (research-only
licence; see THIRD_PARTY.md). The goblin's, checked 2026-10-14 from the workflow ComfyUI
embedded in the PNG: C:\ComfyUI\output\hunyuan3d\goblin_00001_.png, the same file as
C:\Trellis2\inputs\goblin-tpose.png that TRELLIS.2 read (2026-10-09). ComfyUI, models
qwen_image_2512_fp8_e4m3fn.safetensors + Qwen-Image-2512-Lightning-8steps-V1.0-bf16 LoRA
(strength 1.0), text encoder qwen_2.5_vl_7b_fp8_scaled, qwen_image_vae; 1328 x 1328, seed 42,
8 steps, cfg 1.0, euler / simple. Prompt: "low-poly hooded goblin, T-pose. Full body single
character, front view, arms straight out to the sides, centered, flat-shaded low-poly game
asset, plain white background, no shadow, no props, no text". Negative: "multiple characters,
cropped, cut off, background scenery, text, watermark, blurry".

characters/human_m.blend, characters/human_f.blend
                         people's bodies (decisions 243, 388, 419): our own, every vertex placed by
                         tools/body_gen.py on the Quaternius Universal Animation Library's own
                         skeleton (CC0; stood 1.75 m and 1.66 m), no mesh from elsewhere:
                           blender -b -P tools/body_gen.py -- build/ual_extract/UAL1.glb <out>.glb [--sex female]
                         a body (1,526 triangles) and the parts a person may wear, each its own mesh
                         on the skeleton (Hair_Cropped, Hair_Bound, Hair_Long, Beard_Short,
                         Beard_Full, Hood, Hat_Brimmed, Apron), each with seven shape keys (lean,
                         heavy, broad, narrow, hips, old, young: the same parts built again at other
                         proportions); then tools/body_build.py --parts: flat-shaded, one material
                         ("human"), skin, hair, tunic and trousers marked in the vertex colour,
                         belt, boots, hats and the apron on the palette's swatches; the game draws
                         them with characters/human_body.tres. Each .blend's scene property
                         `source` says so.
characters/goblin.blend  generated:trellis2, blender (goblin-4000f.glb: loose parts removed, holes
                         closed, watertight, 1.2 m tall, flat colours; 22-bone humanoid rig with
                         Godot SkeletonProfileHumanoid bone names, T-pose rest, no animations)
                         Repainted by hand: white eye sockets (goblin_eyes), a dark brow under
                         the hood and the mouth (goblin_hair), more of the face and neck skin.
                         Since textured: one material, goblin, showing paint/goblin_paint.png
                         (colour) and paint/goblin_shine.png (roughness, its green) through the
                         UV map "paint"; the seven part materials are kept unused (fake user)
                         to bake from again.
paint/goblin_paint.png   the goblin's colour, 2048 x 2048, painted by hand: a sheet of views
                         (front, back, both sides, from above and below, the face close up from a
                         little below) with the faces no side shows packed in a strip at the foot;
                         each face is in the view it faces and can be seen from, and where two
                         overlap there the nearer keeps it. Started as a bake of the seven parts'
                         colours.
paint/goblin_shine.png   the goblin's roughness on the same sheet (white rough, black shiny):
                         skin 0.55, eyes 0.2, leather 0.5, boots 0.65, cloth 0.9, hood and cape
                         0.95, brow and mouth 0.8; painted by hand from there.
