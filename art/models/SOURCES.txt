The game's 3D models, named by what they are. Meshes generated from a concept image with
TRELLIS.2 (Microsoft, MIT License), using the DINOv3 image encoder (Meta, DINOv3 License) and
BiRefNet background removal (ZhengPeng7, MIT License), then cleaned up, painted and rigged in Blender.
Each .blend records this as the scene custom property `source`.

characters/goblin.blend  generated:trellis2, blender (goblin-4000f.glb: loose parts removed, holes
                         closed, watertight, 1.2 m tall, flat colours; 22-bone humanoid rig with
                         Godot SkeletonProfileHumanoid bone names, T-pose rest, no animations)
characters/goblin.glb    export of goblin.blend: mesh + skin + armature, +Y up

characters/goblin.glb.import  imported with retarget/bone_map = bonemaps/goblin.tres (identity onto
                         SkeletonProfileHumanoid); the skeleton becomes %GeneralSkeleton
bonemaps/goblin.tres, bonemaps/ual.tres
                         bone maps from the retarget test: the goblin (identity) and the Quaternius
                         Universal Animation Library (Unreal mannequin names) onto SkeletonProfileHumanoid
animations/goblin.res    Idle, Walk, Punch_Jab, Hit_Chest, Death01, Jog_Bwd, Hit_Stomach, Roll,
                         Jump_Land from the Quaternius Universal Animation Library (Pro, UAL1.glb,
                         in place), retargeted through bonemaps/ual.tres,
                         saved by tools/extract_clips.gd. Animations by Quaternius, CC0 1.0
                         (animations/LICENSE-quaternius-ual.txt), https://quaternius.com
