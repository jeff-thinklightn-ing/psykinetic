## The pattern

- **[Jeff]** Each area culminates in a companion-centred reward, the way the barrow
  ends in the blessing.
- **[Jeff]** Besides gold and loot, finishing an area affects loyalty and gives a
  companion a permanent ability or trait.
- **[Jeff]** Example being considered: the bow, or archery, learned by a companion at
  an elven outpost in the woods.
- **[proposal]** Three payouts per area: gold and loot (for the town), loyalty (for the
  bond), and a permanent ability (for the companion who was there).
- **[proposal]** The ability is earned by the companion you brought, as with the
  blessing, so who you take where is a real choice.
- **[proposal]** Abilities are taught by a people or a place, not found in chests, so
  each carries the story of where it came from.

## Builds come from history

- **[proposal]** A rolled companion starts as a person (temper, past) and becomes an
  archer, a healer or a shieldbearer through where you took her. Her card records it
  as lived.
- **[proposal]** "Why is Nix your healer?" has a story for an answer.

### Possible teachers (all proposals)

| Where | Who teaches | What |
|---|---|---|
| The barrow | the draugr | the blessing (Jeff; see `crypt.md`) |

### Room

- **[proposal]** The key decision: can a companion learn everything eventually, or
  carry only a few abilities? Limits make companions distinct, make the choice of
  whom to bring matter, and make losing one hurt.
- Open: does each reward open the next area (as the blessing opens the far world), or
  mainly make companions distinct? Possibly both.

## Guides

- **[Jeff]** A companion who has been to a place can guide you there: you follow her
  on autopilot.
- **[proposal]** What she can guide to comes from what she knows: places she has been
  with you (`lived`), and her own birthplace's lands (her rolled card).
- **[proposal]** She walks the real way, on the world's real routes, so the journey
  can be interrupted: she stops when danger shows (a raider camp, a boar) and hands
  control back. Travel is skipped, fights are not.
- **[proposal]** A diegetic stand-in for fast travel: no map teleport, just someone who
  knows the road.
- **[proposal]** One more thing a companion carries: lose her to leaving, and you lose
  her roads.
- **[Jeff]** Next time, she could refuse: it isn't safe, there are bandits on that road.
- **[proposal]** She refuses from what she knows: something she lived through on that
  road, or what she heard (gossip, the road's news, already in the game).
- **[proposal]** Her temper decides how easily: a bold companion goes anyway, a
  cautious one needs the road cleared or a good reason; loyalty can tip it.
- **[proposal]** The world answers it: clear the bandits, and the next time she'll go.
  Her refusal is a quest nobody wrote.

## Loyalty

- **[Jeff]** Areas affect loyalty.
- **[proposal]** Shared history makes loyalty sturdier: blessed together, rescued, a
  gift earned side by side. Long companionship is hardest to break.
- **[proposal]** Loyalty shows in behaviour, not only talk: how close she stays, how
  bravely she fights, what she will touch (a superstitious companion and grave goods).
- Open: does anything on the card feed behaviour yet, or is it all voice so far?
- Possible foundation: the "cheated" standing key on Jeff's waiting list.

## Leaving

From Jeff's med students: a companion should change her behaviour based on what you do,
and could defect from you. Jeff: players will be attached to certain companions.

- **[proposal]** She leaves only because of what the player did, never at random, so
  attachment becomes the reason to treat her well.
- **[proposal]** Warning signs first: she says so, hangs back, sits apart; gossip in
  town says she is unhappy.
- **[proposal]** Steps, not a cliff: cools, refuses, leaves. Rarely, she ends up with
  someone else (the grave robbers, a rival).
- **[proposal]** Usually she goes home to town, cold but findable, with a way back
  through amends.
- **[proposal]** Wren, Nix and Pip (the legacy three) may sulk, refuse or leave for a
  while, but never become enemies.
- Open: if she leaves, does she take what she earned (the blessing, abilities) with her?
- Open: how far can it go: home to town only, or against you?
