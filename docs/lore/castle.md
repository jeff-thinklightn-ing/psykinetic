## Lore team

### The castle

- **[Jeff]** The castle is ancient and dilapidated.
- **[Jeff]** It was built by dwarven stone smiths, plain and utilitarian.
- **[Jeff]** It was built over underground labyrinths, as a cover over a known
  entrance, to guard it.
- **[Jeff]** It guards a treasure that was rumoured and never found.
- **[Jeff]** It is no longer dwarven. It is mostly overrun.
- Open: overrun by whom? (The Blood Rot, their orc or kobold allies, something from
  below, or several at once.)
- **[proposal]** Guarding cuts both ways: keeping treasure hunters out, or keeping
  something in. The dwarves knew which; no one now does.
- **[proposal]** Open: where did the dwarves go?

### The labyrinths

- **[Jeff]** The maps of the labyrinths were lost long ago.
- **[Jeff]** The labyrinths shift: their ways are never the same twice for long.
- **[Jeff]** That is why their treasures were never carried out, and why adventurers
  still come to this day.
- **[proposal]** The labyrinths are older than the castle. Who made them is open.
- **[proposal]** Why they shift is open. Jeff suggests magic may be at work (touches
  B2). Possibilities: the labyrinths were made that way; the fallen star's influence
  reaches them; something below moves them.

### What lies below

- **[Jeff]** Dwarven artifacts come from the dungeons below the castle.
- **[proposal]** Dwarven work as an item style: plain, heavy, sound, made to last,
  in contrast to the dark elven work from the tower.

### Others who delve

- **[Jeff]** Adventurers still come to the labyrinths to this day.
- **[proposal]** Other delvers are part of the living world: parties going down,
  some never coming back, their gear found later, their names in the town's talk.

## Map team

- **[Jeff]** The overworld stays consistent.
- **[Jeff]** The labyrinths under the castle are the delve-again places: they make
  new maps fairly often.
- **[Jeff]** The tower's inside shifts too (see `blood_rot.md`).
- **[proposal]** Shifting halls, fixed anchors. The castle gate, the castle's upper
  keep, and key rooms (a dwarven gatehouse below, a deepest chamber) stay put, so
  story beats and landmarks hold while the rest regenerates.
- **[proposal]** The castle itself (above ground) is part of the consistent
  overworld; the shifting starts below it.
- **[proposal]** How often maps regenerate ties to the world's slow tick.

## Art team

- **[Jeff]** Dwarven work: plain, heavy, built to last. Contrast with the dark
  elven tower, which is majestic.
- **[Jeff]** Dilapidated and overrun.
- **[proposal]** Dwarven stonework underneath, the overrunners' mess on top, the
  same layering as the tower, so each place shows who built it and who holds it now.
- **[proposal]** Below, a third style for the labyrinths themselves, older than the
